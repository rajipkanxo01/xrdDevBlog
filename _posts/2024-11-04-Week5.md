<b>Starting VR Development</b>

This week was the beginning of development for our VR application!, we are starting initial planning and development for our idea!

### Idea & workflow

Our idea for a VR application is a Interactive Maze, that works similarly to a Escape Room. Where the user must avoid traps, and intereract with various puzzles in order to progress and escape the maze.
We will be breaking down the work with the following features

<b> Maze Map </b>: This is the primary part of the map and its design, this is where the user will be interacting with puzzles and avoid traps.

<b> Traps </b>: A system in which a player is hit by a trap (ex. pitfall trap) and causes the player to start from the beginning or take damage.

<b> Puzzles </b>: Simple puzzles that will block of area of progression or rewards, these must be complete in order to progress further.

<b> Player Movement </b>: This is how the player will move and interact with their enviroment. This is necessary not only to decide how the player should be able to interact with their enviroment but also how
locomotion will occur.


#### Starting Development

we kicked off by meeting and deviding work where it was reasonable, starting with play movement, the maze map, and the traps & puzzles being devided up. 
We spent the day learning how the VR headsets worked and understanding how the player interacts with the device and their movements inside a mocked enviroment.
